An absolute fun read! A beautiful journey of building a computer (a reduced Intel 8080) from scratch, written in a manner that is accessible even to high schoolers without compromising on complexity. This wasn't my first introduction to computer architecture, but I wish it was. The flashlight problem setup it opens with is so versatile, easily motivating all the big ideas around codes, counting, and circuits. I especially loved exploring these lower-level hardware tech, as well as the scattered historical tidbits along the way.\
\
While the book succeeds in its initial goal of building a computer, I wish it had given similar love to "how-to" actually use the computer we built (basically the applications) though it is understandable why that wasn't done.
