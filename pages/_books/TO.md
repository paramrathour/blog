A grounded story with good life lessons. It's just that a story revolving around juvenile delinquents wasn't really for me, so it didn't hit as hard. It also ended up feeling tropey, but that's likely because it was the progenitor of many of the teenage rebellion stories that followed. The ending is nice, but it has also become a popular trope over the years.
