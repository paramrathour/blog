It's funny describing a math book like this, but let's try.\
\
This novelette takes a good chunk of time to set up (about 80%), and it is really necessary to wrap your head around Conway's system of numbers. But once that's done, the dominoes topple one after another at a faster and faster pace, eventually reaching infinity and then going past it, xD.\
\
The payoff is truly a surreal experience, but as they say, it's not about the destination but about the journey.
