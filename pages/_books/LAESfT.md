First and foremost, amazing artwork. A treat to look at!\
Secondly, I particularly enjoyed that it didn't shy away from showing the flaws of these math giants and just how much of a mess they were. A much better take than blindly idealising them. The contrast between Russell/Wittgenstein was also handled well.\
\
It's not like the rest of the book is bad, but IMO, its potential was far higher than what it was able to achieve. It tries to be both a novel and an explainer, but I don't think it was able to do justice to either in such a short story. A longer version might have helped flesh out both the characters and the math/philosophical concepts.\
\
Though I am curious to hear what people who didn't already know about this foundational quest in mathematics think of the book. Maybe they will enjoy it more.\
\
Also, check out the comic Orb: On the Movements of the Earth. It does this kind of science + philosophy + history amazingly well. If I hadn't read it, I probably wouldn't have known how high the bar for a book like this could be.
